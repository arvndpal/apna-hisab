Meaning rules

Income is always green with a “+” and a ↙ in-arrow. Expense is always burnt-orange with a “−” and a ↗ out-arrow. Net takes the colour of its sign. Udhaar uses indigo for people, with green “Receive” and orange “Pay”. Because green and orange sit at similar lightness, sign and arrow are mandatory — colour alone never carries meaning (colour-blind safe). In charts, expense bars use the lighter orange #E8875B so the pair also differs in lightness.
